# Ethics, Privacy, and Responsible Use

RespiratoryVision is a **research/educational prototype** and not a medical device.

## Privacy

RGB video can contain directly identifiable biometric information. Any real-person recording should be processed only with appropriate consent, authorization, storage controls, and institutional policies.

Recommended practices:
- Avoid committing identifiable videos to Git.
- Keep private recordings outside the repository.
- Store only derived signals when possible.
- Minimize retained metadata.
- Document data provenance and permitted use.
- Apply access controls to sensitive research data.

## Human-participant research

Work involving participants or clinical datasets may require:
- institutional research ethics review,
- informed consent,
- privacy/security assessment,
- data-use agreements,
- approved retention/deletion procedures.

Completing a research-ethics course does not itself authorize human-participant research.

## Bias and validity

Performance may vary with:
- body position,
- camera viewpoint,
- clothing,
- lighting,
- movement,
- occlusion,
- age/body size,
- frame rate and compression.

Any healthcare deployment would require rigorous prospective validation and clinically appropriate reference measurements.

## Claims

The included synthetic results demonstrate code behavior under controlled conditions only. They must not be represented as patient, pediatric, diagnostic, or clinical performance.
